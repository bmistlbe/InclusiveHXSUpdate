We have presented updated LHC Higgs Working Group recommendations for
the inclusive production cross sections of a Standard Model Higgs
boson at $\sqrt{s}=7,\,8,\,13,\,13.6$, and $14$~TeV, covering gluon
fusion, vector-boson fusion, associated production with a vector
boson, associated production with top quarks, single-top associated
production, and associated production with bottom quarks. This update
supersedes the recommendations of Yellow Report~4
(YR4)~\cite{deFlorian:2016spz} and the ad interim interpolation of
Ref.~\cite{Karlberg:2024zxx}, incorporating close to a decade of
theoretical progress into a single, consistently defined reference.

For every production mode considered, the resulting central
predictions are consistent with those of YR4 within the quoted
uncertainties. At the same time, the precision of the predictions has
improved markedly for most channels, driven by the availability of new
higher-order QCD corrections -- including N$^3$LO results for gluon
fusion and vector-boson fusion, NNLO~+~NNLL soft-gluon resummation
matched with NLO electroweak corrections for $t\bar{t}H$, and
N$^3$LO/NLO flavour-scheme-matched predictions for $b\bar{b}H$ --
together with improved electroweak corrections, refined treatments of
finite-quark-mass effects, and updated global PDF sets. As a
consequence, the total theoretical uncertainty (neglecting PDF
uncertainties) on most production cross sections is now smaller than,
or comparable to, the projected precision of Higgs-boson
signal-strength measurements at the HL-LHC, underlining the importance
of these developments for fully exploiting the physics potential of
the high-luminosity programme.

Parton distribution functions remain the dominant source of
uncertainty across essentially all production modes, and constitute
the leading bottleneck for further improvement. For gluon fusion in
particular, the persistent tension between NNLO and approximate
N$^3$LO PDF sets, and between the aN$^3$LO fits of different
collaborations, has led us to double the PDF-theory uncertainty
relative to YR4 in order to conservatively bracket this mismatch. We
have shown that this discrepancy would be substantially reduced by
consistent use of N$^3$LO-order PDFs, and we have additionally
quantified the impact of QED corrections to the DGLAP evolution,
which can shift the gluon-fusion cross section by up to about $1.7\%$
depending on the PDF set and collider energy. These observations
highlight the need for continued progress -- and eventual community
consensus -- on N$^3$LO PDF determinations ahead of the HL-LHC era.

The numerical predictions, together with a full breakdown of the
associated theoretical and parametric uncertainties, are tabulated in
Appendix~\ref{app:tables} for all production modes, collider energies,
and Higgs-boson masses considered. We hope that this report will
serve, as YR4 did previously, as the standard reference for
Higgs-boson production cross sections at the LHC and HL-LHC, and we
anticipate that further updates will be required as new perturbative
calculations, improved PDF sets, and refined uncertainty estimates
become available.
