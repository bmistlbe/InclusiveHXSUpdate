
The comparison plots presented in this section illustrate the evolution of the theoretical uncertainties on the Higgs boson production cross sections between Yellow Report 4 and the current recommendations described in this report, and place these results in the context of the projected experimental precision at the HL-LHC. They demonstrate the significant reduction in theoretical uncertainties achieved through advances in higher-order calculations, improvements in PDFs, and refined treatments of theoretical and parametric uncertainties. HL-LHC projection studies by ATLAS and CMS show that further reductions in theoretical uncertainties will be important for fully exploiting the precision potential of the HL-LHC. Optimistic projection scenarios typically assume theory uncertainties reduced by approximately a factor of two relative to those of Yellow Report 4. The recommendations presented in this report represent the current state of the art. Continued progress in higher-order perturbative calculations, PDF determinations, and the assessment of residual theoretical uncertainties will be essential to match the experimental precision expected from the HL-LHC program.
