
Figure~\ref{fig:YR5_summary_uncertainties} illustrates the evolution of the theoretical uncertainties on the Higgs boson production cross sections between YR4 and the current recommendations described in this report, and places these results in the context of the most precise LHC Run 2 Higgs boson signal strength measurements $\mu = \sigma/\sigma_{SM}$~\cite{ATLAS:2025qxq,CMS:2026nce}, and their projected precision at the HL-LHC~\cite{ATLAS:2022hsp}. The corresponding numerical values are summarised in Table~\ref{tab:prod_uncertainties}. Figure~\ref{fig:YR5_ggfsummary_uncertainties} focuses on the ggF production mode and shows the breakdown of the individual contributions to its total theoretical uncertainty. These comparisons demonstrate the significant reduction in theoretical uncertainties achieved through advances in higher-order calculations, improvements in PDFs, and refined treatments of theoretical and parametric uncertainties. HL-LHC projection studies by ATLAS and CMS show that further reductions in theoretical uncertainties will be important for fully exploiting the precision potential of the HL-LHC. Optimistic projection scenarios typically assume theory uncertainties reduced by approximately a factor of two relative to those of YR4. The recommendations presented in this report represent the current state of the art. Continued progress in higher-order perturbative calculations, PDF determinations, and the assessment of residual theoretical uncertainties will be essential to match the experimental precision expected from the HL-LHC program.
