\subsection{Summary of Theory Developments}
\label{sec:vbf_theory}

Vector-boson fusion (VBF) Higgs production is most naturally described as
part of the electroweak production of a Higgs boson in association with two
jets. The full electroweak $Hjj$ process contains $s$-, $t$- and $u$-channel
topologies and their interferences. The VBF approximation isolates the
dominant VBF-like contribution, while contributions associated with
Higgs-strahlung topologies and interferences between the different
topologies are neglected. The quality of this approximation has been studied
extensively
\cite{Figy:2003nv,Berger:2004pca,Ciccolini:2007jr,Bolzoni:2011cu,
Campanario:2013fsa,Campanario:2018ppz,Barone:2025jey}.

Within the structure-function, or DIS, approximation, QCD corrections
factorise into corrections associated with the two quark lines. The
inclusive QCD cross section is known through
N$^3$LO~\cite{Dreyer:2016oyx}, while differential predictions are available
through NNLO QCD~\cite{Cacciari:2015jma,Cruz-Martinez:2018rod}. The
N$^3$LO calculation exhibits very good perturbative convergence and a small
residual scale dependence~\cite{Dreyer:2016oyx}. At this level of precision,
effects that are not captured by the structure-function approximation become
increasingly relevant.

Non-factorisable QCD corrections arise from colour exchange between the two
quark lines and have been investigated in
Refs.~\cite{Liu:2019tuy,Dreyer:2020urf,Asteriadis:2023nyl,
Bronnum-Hansen:2023vzh,Long:2023mvc,Gates:2023iiv}. The available
calculations rely on an eikonal expansion applicable in VBF-like kinematics,
but this approximation is not controlled over the fully inclusive phase
space considered for the cross sections in this report. We therefore do not
apply the calculated non-factorisable contribution as a correction to the
central prediction, but use its size to estimate the parametric magnitude of
non-factorisable effects missing from the structure-function approximation.

A separate issue concerns contributions involving the $s$-, $t$- and
$u$-channel topologies and their interferences. These can be evaluated using
the full electroweak $Hjj$ matrix element, but beyond the VBF approximation
their assignment to either the VBF or VH production mode is not unambiguous.
Since VBF and VH are quoted as separate production modes in this report, we
do not assign these contributions to either central prediction. Their size
is instead used to estimate the uncertainty associated with the
structure-function approximation and the separation of the inclusive
electroweak Higgs-production cross section into VBF and VH components.

Electroweak corrections are known through NLO
\cite{Ciccolini:2007jr,Ciccolini:2007ec} and are included in the
recommendation. The photon-initiated contribution is also included in the
central prediction. As described in Section~\ref{sec:pdf}, we use the
LUXqed17\_plus\_PDF4LHC15\_nnlo\_100 photon distribution for this
contribution, while retaining PDF4LHC21 for the quark and gluon
distributions. This constitutes a hybrid prescription, since the
corresponding QED evolution of the quark and gluon PDFs is not included.
The numerical impact of this approximation and its treatment in the
uncertainty prescription are discussed in Section~\ref{sec:pdf}.

Considerable progress has also been made for differential and exclusive VBF
predictions. This includes studies of higher-multiplicity and irreducible
background contributions
\cite{Andersen:2006ag,Andersen:2007mp,Harlander:2008xn,Greiner:2016awe,
Greiner:2015jha,Andersen:2017kfc,Andersen:2018tnm,Andersen:2018kjg,
Chen:2021azt,Andersen:2022zte}, boosted-Higgs production
\cite{Becker:2020rjp,Buckley:2021gfw}, and the interplay of Higgs production
and decay~\cite{Asteriadis:2021gpd,Asteriadis:2024nbg}. NLO QCD corrections
have been matched to parton showers~\cite{Nason:2009ai,Frixione:2013mta},
with NLO electroweak effects also incorporated in matched simulations
\cite{Jager:2022acp}. Multi-jet merging has been studied in
Refs.~\cite{Hoche:2021mkv,Chen:2021phj}, while uncertainties associated with
NLO QCD plus parton-shower simulations have been investigated in
Refs.~\cite{Jager:2020hkz,Buckley:2021gfw,Hoche:2021mkv}.
Non-perturbative effects from multi-parton interactions and hadronisation
have also been studied~\cite{Hoche:2021mkv,Bittrich:2021ztq}, together with
developments towards next-to-leading-logarithmic parton showers for
VBF~\cite{vanBeekveld:2023chs}.

For a recent comprehensive study and review of VBF phenomenology, including
recommendations for uncertainties in parton-shower simulations, we refer to
Ref.~\cite{Barone:2025jey}. The recent Les Houches
wishlist~\cite{Huss:2025nlt} provides a complementary overview of
fixed-order developments.

\subsection{Inclusive Cross Section Predictions}
\label{sec:vbf_xsec}

The inclusive VBF predictions collected in
Tables~\ref{tab:vbf_XStot_7}--\ref{tab:vbf_XStot_14} are constructed as
\begin{align}
  \label{eq:vbf-xsec-combined}
  \sigma^{\VBF}
  =
  \sigma_{\NNNLO}^{\DIS}(1+\delta_{\ELWK})
  + \sigma_{\gamma},
\end{align}
where $\sigma_{\NNNLO}^{\DIS}$ is the N$^3$LO QCD cross section in the
structure-function (DIS) approximation and $\delta_{\ELWK}$ denotes the
relative NLO electroweak correction excluding photon-initiated
contributions. The electroweak corrections and photon-initiated contribution
are evaluated with \textsc{HAWK}~\cite{Denner:2014cla}. While
\textsc{HAWK} includes all relevant partonic channels at LO, channels with
bottom quarks in the external state are omitted at NLO; their numerical
impact is expected to be small.

The photon-initiated contribution $\sigma_{\gamma}$ is added separately and
is evaluated using the hybrid PDF prescription described in
Section~\ref{sec:pdf}: specifically the photon distribution is taken from
\texttt{LUXqed17\_plus\_PDF4LHC15\_nnlo\_100}, while PDF4LHC21 is retained
for the quark and gluon distributions.

The theory uncertainty accounts for missing higher-order electroweak
corrections and for effects not included in the structure-function
approximation. For $\sqrt{s}=13$, $13.6$ and $14$~TeV, it is defined as
\begin{align}
  \label{eq:vbf-TU}
  \Delta_{\mathrm{TU}}
  =
  \max\left\{0.5\%,\delta_{\ELWK}^2\right\}
  +
  \frac{
    |\sigma_{\mbox{\scriptsize nf}}|
    +
    |\sigma_{\mbox{\scriptsize s/t/u}}|
  }{\sigma^{\VBF}}\% .
\end{align}
Here $\delta_{\ELWK}$ is understood as a dimensionless relative correction.
The first term follows the prescription adopted in
YR4~\cite{LHCHiggsCrossSectionWorkingGroup:2016ypw}: the $0.5\%$ floor
provides a generic estimate of missing higher-order electroweak effects,
while $\delta_{\ELWK}^2$ allows for a larger uncertainty when the known NLO
electroweak correction is sizeable.

The term $\sigma_{\mbox{\scriptsize nf}}$ is used to estimate the
parametric size of non-factorisable QCD effects omitted by the
structure-function approximation. As discussed in
Section~\ref{sec:vbf_theory}, the available calculations rely on an
eikonal expansion applicable in VBF-like kinematics, but this approximation
is not controlled over the fully inclusive phase space considered here.
We therefore use the magnitude of the calculated contribution as an
uncertainty estimate rather than applying it as a correction to the central
cross section.

The quantity $\sigma_{\mbox{\scriptsize s/t/u}}$ quantifies contributions
involving the $s$-, $t$- and $u$-channel topologies and their interferences.
These contributions can be evaluated using the full electroweak $Hjj$
matrix element, but beyond the VBF approximation their assignment to either
the VBF or VH production mode is not unambiguous. Since VBF and VH are
quoted as separate production modes in this report, we do not assign these
contributions to either central prediction. Their magnitude is instead used
to estimate the uncertainty associated with the structure-function
approximation and with the separation of the inclusive electroweak
Higgs-production cross section into VBF and VH components.

For the legacy predictions at $\sqrt{s}=7$ and $8$~TeV, the corresponding
non-factorisable estimate is not available. We therefore impose a
conservative minimum theory uncertainty of $1\%$,
\begin{align}
  \label{eq:vbf-TU-legacy}
  \Delta_{\mathrm{TU}}
  =
  \max\left[
    \max\left\{0.5\%,\delta_{\ELWK}^2\right\}
    +
    \frac{|\sigma_{\mbox{\scriptsize s/t/u}}|}
         {\sigma^{\VBF}}\%,
    1.0\%
  \right].
\end{align}
For the predictions considered here, the $1\%$ floor determines
$\Delta_{\mathrm{TU}}$ at both energies.

The PDF and $\alpha_s$ uncertainties are evaluated according to the common
prescription of Section~\ref{sec:pdf}. As discussed there, the hybrid
treatment used for VBF includes the photon-initiated contribution but not
the separate effect of QED evolution on the quark and gluon distributions.
The estimated QED-evolution effect is subdominant to the present uncertainty
budget, and no additional uncertainty is assigned to the hybrid
photon-PDF prescription.

The impact of using approximate N$^3$LO PDFs for VBF has been studied by
the MSHT and NNPDF collaborations and in their subsequent combined
analysis~\cite{McGowan:2022nag,NNPDF:2024nan,Cridge:2024icl}. These studies
find an upward shift of the VBF cross section when replacing NNLO by
aN$^3$LO PDFs. The size of the shift depends on the PDF determination, with
more recent determinations and their combination favouring a somewhat
smaller effect than the original MSHT20aN$^3$LO result, while remaining
comparable to the PDF+$\alpha_s$ uncertainty of the present prediction.
In contrast to gluon fusion, where the corresponding effect substantially
exceeds the PDF uncertainty, we do not introduce an additional PDF-order
uncertainty for VBF. The baseline prediction therefore continues to use the
common PDF4LHC21 NNLO prescription of Section~\ref{sec:pdf}; the general
status of PDFs beyond NNLO is discussed in
Section~\ref{sec:an3lo-pdfs}.

\paragraph{Recommendation}
The inclusive VBF cross section is evaluated at N$^3$LO QCD in the
structure-function approximation and supplemented multiplicatively by the
NLO electroweak correction and additively by the photon-initiated
contribution, according to Eq.~\eqref{eq:vbf-xsec-combined}. The theory
uncertainty $\Delta_{\mathrm{TU}}$ is evaluated using
Eq.~\eqref{eq:vbf-TU} at $\sqrt{s}=13$, $13.6$ and $14$~TeV, and
Eq.~\eqref{eq:vbf-TU-legacy} at $\sqrt{s}=7$ and $8$~TeV. PDF and
$\alpha_s$ uncertainties follow the common PDF4LHC21 prescription; no
additional PDF-order or QED-evolution uncertainty is assigned.
